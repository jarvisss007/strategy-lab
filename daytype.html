🎚️ Day-Type Radar

The SNDK day-type framework applied to the whole universe — which names offer intraday opportunity, and whether they trend or chop.
path% = total intraday distance traveled as a share of price (the opportunity — high = lots of movement to trade). eff = efficiency (net move ÷ path): high = it trends, low = it chops. Character: TRENDY (eff ≥ 0.45), CHOPPY (eff < 0.30), BALANCED between, and QUIET = too little intraday movement to day-trade or classify (most of the universe). Sorted by opportunity. Reliable read: path% is the opportunity metric; trend/chop character only means something for non-QUIET names. This describes intraday behavior for sizing and attention — not a signal or an edge.
