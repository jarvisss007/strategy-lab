How to read this. Every strategy family was tested across its whole parameter grid. Deflated Sharpe discounts the best config for how many were tried — ≥ 0.95 means significant after that penalty. PBO is the probability the in-sample winner underperforms out of sample — > 0.5 is a red flag. OOS Sharpe is the winner's Sharpe on the untouched last 30% of history. A real edge needs a high Deflated Sharpe, a low PBO, and an OOS Sharpe that survives. The Day-of-week row is a deliberate noise control — it should be rejected.
