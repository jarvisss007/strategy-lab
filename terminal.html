What each learning loop is waiting on

The Position Agent — Mon/Wed/Fri, paper-only, equity, technical only (mandate changed 2026-07-31). Theses rest on price structure alone — trend, range location, breakouts, pullbacks, realized volatility, day-type character. Fundamentals, the Graham gate, and insider activity are explicitly excluded; earnings are a blackout, never a signal. Stop and target are drawn off structure (swing levels / realized daily move) with reward:risk ≥ 1.5 required, all predefined BEFORE entry (RULES.md Rule 1, enforced in code). Grading is real: the daily price path is walked for stop/target breaches, benchmarked against SPY over the identical window. Honest prior: the returns-matrix study already grades momentum weak and reversal sub-cost — this re-tests convicted signals under stricter execution, not a new idea. Positions opened before 2026-07-31 (graham, insider tags) are legacy and kept unaltered; they are reported separately, never averaged in. Options remain out of scope until a real pricing feed exists — see agent/POSITION_AGENT.md.

The Paper Arena — the agents actually trade here. Twelve fixed rules (registered in REGISTRY.md with theses, never tuned after registration; the 4-rule tempo tier joined 2026-07-25) take paper positions automatically every refresh: entry/exit at the signal close, 10 bps per side, one position per name per rule, max 40 concurrent per rule, every trade benchmarked against SPY and tagged with the regime at entry (calm/storm = VIX </≥ 20 · up/down = SPY vs 50d MA). The 1-year replay is instant analysis; the live forward book is the record that actually counts.
