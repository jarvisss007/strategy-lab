## Why this file exists

The forward book will, in a few months, show numbers. Whatever they are, a
motivated reader (including us) will be able to tell a story about them. This
file removes that freedom now: it says in advance what counts as a pass, a fail,
and a non-answer, so October's verdict is read against August's words.

## 1 · When the forward book earns the right to speak

Not before **60 distinct entry days** in a branch's book. Below that, every
statistic is weather. (Today: US 25, India 3.) Rows never count; days do.

## 2 · The pass bar, per family or arm

A family/arm PASSES its forward test only if, at ≥60 entry days:
- day-clustered mean excess vs its benchmark (SPY / NIFTY) is **> 2 × its
  day-clustered standard error**, and
- it survives the carried deflation budget (the US registry's ~35-trial count
  carries over; India extends the sample, never resets the clock), and
- the same number remains positive after 2×COST per round trip.

Anything less at 60+ days → **UNPROVEN stands**. A negative day-clustered mean
at 60+ days → the family is **RETIRED from forward entry** (it keeps trading in
replay as a control, never in the forward book).

## 3 · Overlay verdicts (rotation · regime-exit · stop-only)

Each is judged at its registered **40 sessions** on overlay-NAV minus base-NAV:
- gap > +2 × day-clustered SE → ADOPTED (the base book changes — the only
  mechanism by which any live rule of this desk may ever change);
- gap within ±2 SE → the overlay is DROPPED as unproven complexity;
- gap < −2 SE → dropped, and its registered prediction is marked CONFIRMED.
One down-week favoring stop-only (as 2026-08-19 does) is weather, not verdict.

## 4 · The desk-level expected outcome, stated before the data

The backtest's own verdict predicts the forward book: **excess decays toward
zero as the entry window diversifies beyond the July 2026 dip-recovery tape.**
If the pooled day-clustered excess at 60+ days is indistinguishable from zero,
that is the expected result and will be reported as CONFIRMATION of the
deflation gate — not as failure. If a family passes §2 instead, the desk says
so with equal volume.

## 5 · What no result can do

No forward result, however good, reopens Rule 7 by itself. The Constitution's
live-capital gate (40 journaled sim trades with stops, targets, and sizes) is a
statement about the OPERATOR, not the strategies, and this file cannot touch it.
